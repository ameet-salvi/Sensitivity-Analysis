# Microsoft Integrated 3-Statement Valuation & Share Price Sensitivity Engine

## Executive Overview

This project represents the development of a fully integrated institutional-grade financial valuation model for Microsoft, designed to simulate enterprise valuation dynamics under multiple macroeconomic and operational scenarios.

The model has been engineered with the philosophy of combining:

* Investment Banking rigor
* Corporate FP&A intelligence
* Strategic valuation analytics
* Executive-level visualization design

The outcome is a dynamic financial intelligence framework capable of translating operational assumptions directly into enterprise value and implied share price movements.

---

## Strategic Objective

The purpose of this model is not merely forecasting.

It is designed to replicate the analytical architecture used by:

* Global Investment Banks
* Private Equity Funds
* Equity Research Desks
* Sovereign Wealth Funds
* Corporate Strategy Divisions
* CFO Transformation Offices

The model enables leadership teams to visualize how micro-level operational changes cascade through the financial statements and ultimately impact market capitalization and shareholder value.

# Advanced DCF Valuation Architecture

The valuation engine incorporates:

* Multi-year discounted cash flow modeling
* Dynamic WACC assumptions
* Terminal value modeling
* EV / EBITDA exit multiple methodology
* Equity bridge calculations
* Implied share price derivation

The framework is structured to emulate institutional equity research methodologies used across Tier-1 financial institutions.

---

# Share Price Sensitivity Intelligence Layer

One of the most powerful components of the project is the interactive valuation sensitivity engine.

The model stress-tests implied share price movements against changes in:

* Revenue Growth Rate
* Gross Margin / COGS %
* EBITDA Margin
* Discount Rate (WACC)
* EV / EBITDA Exit Multiple
* Tax Rate
* Capital Expenditure Intensity

This transforms the workbook from a static financial model into a strategic decision intelligence platform.
